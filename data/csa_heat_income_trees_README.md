# CSA Heat, Income, and Tree Coverage Data

`csa_heat_income_trees.csv` contains one row for each of Baltimore's 55 named
Community Statistical Areas (CSAs). It combines:

- Mean modeled temperature from the Baltimore temperature-surface rasters
- Median household income from the CSA income dataset
- Percent of CSA area covered by trees

The primary join key is `csa2010`. The heat calculation uses the CSA polygons
in [`Community_Statistical_Areas_(CSAs)__Reference_Boundaries.geojson`](Community_Statistical_Areas_(CSAs)__Reference_Boundaries.geojson),
and the income and tree tables are joined to those polygons through their
`CSA2010` values.

## Columns

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| `csa2010` | Community Statistical Area name from the 2010 naming scheme. Primary key and join field. |
| `csa2020` | Corresponding Community Statistical Area name from the 2020 naming scheme. Some areas were renamed between 2010 and 2020. |
| `temp_am_mean` | Mean value of `bal_am.tif` inside the CSA. This represents modeled morning temperature, approximately 6 AM. |
| `temp_af_mean` | Mean value of `bal_af.tif` inside the CSA. This represents modeled afternoon temperature, approximately 3 PM. |
| `temp_pm_mean` | Mean value of `bal_pm.tif` inside the CSA. This represents modeled evening temperature, approximately 7 PM. |
| `mhhi17` | Median household income for the 2017 income year, reported in dollars. |
| `mhhi23` | Median household income for the 2023 income year, reported in dollars. |
| `trees11` | Percent of the CSA's area covered by trees in 2011. |
| `trees17` | Percent of the CSA's area covered by trees in 2017. |

## Important notes

- The `temp_*` columns are zonal means calculated from the source rasters.
- The TIFF metadata does not declare temperature units. Keep these values in
  the source-raster units unless the original temperature-data documentation
  confirms a conversion.
- The heat rasters are `bal_am.tif`, `bal_af.tif`, and `bal_pm.tif` in
  [`baltimore/temperature surfaces`](baltimore/temperature%20surfaces).
- The `Unassigned -- Jail` polygon is excluded because it has no corresponding
  income or tree-coverage row.
- The CSV was generated by
  [`scripts/merge_csa_datasets.py`](../scripts/merge_csa_datasets.py).
